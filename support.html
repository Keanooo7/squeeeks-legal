Common questions

I bought something and did not receive it

Leave the Shop open for a moment — purchases are confirmed by our server and appear on their own, with no need to refresh. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date and time of the purchase and we will look it up.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same account, open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Restores are tied to the Apple ID that made the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the period ends stops the next renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

My streak reset and I do not think it should have

Streaks roll over at 4 AM in your device's local time, not midnight — so a late-night cleaning session still counts for the day you started it. If you travelled across time zones or changed your device clock, the streak may have advanced early. Email us with the dates and we can check the record.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Account & Data → Delete account. This removes your account and its associated data, including the family chat messages you sent. See the Privacy Policy for what we store and why.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Apple keeps billing until you cancel. Cancel first in the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.

I signed up with Sign in with Apple

Sign in again with Apple on any device to get your account back. If you chose Hide My Email, our emails reach you through Apple's relay address. Support emails you send us come from your real address, so tell us your display name and we can find the account.

I lost progress on my house or my collection

Your house layout and collection are saved to your account, not to the device, so signing in on any device should bring them back. Make sure you are signed in with the same method you originally used. If something is genuinely missing, email us as soon as you notice — the sooner we look, the more we can recover.

Reporting a bug or a player

Send bug reports to the address above.

To report a player or a family chat message, use Report on the message or on the player in the app. To stop a player contacting you, use Block; they can no longer send you friend requests, gifts or family messages. You can also email us with their display name and what happened. We read every report, and accounts that break the Terms of Service can have content removed, or be suspended or removed.
