Families and other players

A family is a small group, up to five people including whoever started it, that shares one chore board. People join a family with an invite from the person who started it. The person who started it can hand out chores, plan each member's week and remove members. Everyone in the family can see which chores each member has and when they finish them, and can see each member's house.

Families have no typed chat. Members cheer each other on by giving kudos or sending one of the app's stickers on a chore. Apart from display names, the family's name and the nicknames of any plants shared with the family, nothing anyone types is shown to the family. The family's name is set by the person who started it and checked by an automatic word filter.

Whoever plans chores for others is responsible for keeping them safe and suitable for the person doing them, especially for younger members.

If another player bothers you, use Report to tell us and Block to stop that player contacting you. You can also email us.
